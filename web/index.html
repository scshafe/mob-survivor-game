How to play
  • Slide your cannon with A/D or the arrows; hold Space to fire. (On a phone: drag, and hold to fire.)
  • Gates change every mob that runs through: ×N multiplies, +N adds, ÷2 culls half. Purple-edged gates (⚔) help both sides.
  • Some gates change what your mobs are: 10→👑 fuses every 10 into a giant, »RUN» makes them fast runners, ⛨ ARMOR lets each one shrug off its next hit.
  • Mobs that touch enemies trade blows one for one. Reach the far wall to damage the base.
  • Firing charges a Giant: launch it with G (or E, or the crown button). It tramples whole squads.
  • Bomb: B (or Q) shows the aimer on the nearest enemies; move it vim-style with hjkl (a count repeats: 5k), n/N for the next enemy group farther/nearer, 0/$ for the walls, H/M/L for top/middle/bottom; B or Enter throws, Esc cancels. (On a phone: tap the bomb button, then the field.)
  • Phase (F): for 5 s your mobs ignore saw blades and ÷2 gates but still fight; it recharges in 20 s.
  • Orbs drift across the lane: break one with your mobs to claim it. Gold +1: one more mob every volley for the rest of the level (up to three). Blue ❄: enemies march at half speed. Pink ÷2: every gate halves the enemy. White 🛡: your base soaks up damage. Green 🧲: your mobs drift into the gates.
  • From level 3 a level may have a named layout: Hourglass walls pinch the middle, Twin Lanes split it in two, Conveyor belts carry mobs sideways, Teleporters swap them across.
  • Watch out for saw blades, bosses every fifth level, and the Frenzy at 2:30 in versus.
  • Between campaign levels, each player picks one of three upgrade cards.
